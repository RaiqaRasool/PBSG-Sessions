# From résumé to portfolio with AI

Codex is the AI coding assistant in the ChatGPT desktop app. You’ll give it your résumé, talk through a design, and ask it to build a website you can open in your browser.

1. **[Start the workshop guide](participant-kit/START-HERE.md)** to prepare your résumé, open a local folder in Codex, and copy the build and update prompts.
2. **[Jump to Codex setup](participant-kit/START-HERE.md#2-open-your-folder-in-codex)** if your résumé is ready. The facilitator will walk through the desktop screens during the session.
3. **[Publish your site if you want a public link](participant-kit/PUBLISH.md)** at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/`.

You can also [download the guides and fictional sample résumé](portfolio-starter.zip) for offline reference.
