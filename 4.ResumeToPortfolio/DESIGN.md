## Established intent
- Express personality and make the portfolio enjoyable to explore.
- Keep the person's work and visitor needs central.
- Make the result readable, responsive, and accessible.
- Choose section names and hierarchy based on actual content.

## Decisions to make
- Primary audience and main visitor action.
- Overall mood, typography, palette, composition, and motion level.
- Selected work and the amount of detail each item deserves.
- Available portraits, scientific figures, project images, or other personal assets.

## Reference record
For each selected reference, record:
- URL or local screenshot path.
- Specific qualities to borrow, such as typography, spacing, palette, or an interaction.
- How those qualities support this portfolio's content.
- Any asset attribution or reuse requirements.
